# Prompts das três propostas

Geradas em 18/10/2026 com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen (sem CLI/API externa). São estudos de direção artística, não capturas do jogo nem assets prontos de produção. Geometria, quantidade de peças e marcações desenhadas pela IA não especificam regras. As três imagens foram inspecionadas visualmente.

Arquivos: `01-heranca-premium.png`, `02-arena-contemporanea.png`, `03-mesas-do-mundo.png`.

## Proposta 1

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: art direction exploration for BFC, a Brazilian button football video game for worldwide players. Create one polished wide landscape art direction board, 16:9. This is FUTEBOL DE BOTAO: actual small low circular acrylic BUTTON DISCS slid with a plastic plectrum across a SMOOTH HARD TABLE painted green with white pitch markings. No human footballers, no legs, no foosball rods, no upright miniature figurines, no grass blades, no giant stadium field masquerading as a table. Each outfield piece is a low disc with beveled edges, diameter about five times its thickness, printed abstract team insignia under glossy acrylic; goalkeepers are simple squat rectangular blocks. Small pale ball much smaller than discs. No copyrighted clubs or sponsor marks. Board composition: a large central 3/4 elevated gameplay concept showing readable separated opposing discs and two miniature goals, a lower strip with a macro cosmetic disc material study and a small goal celebration VFX study, elegant restrained graphic layout. Only text allowed: tiny BFC in a corner; no paragraphs. It is aspirational concept art, not a screenshot of an existing game. Competition view must keep ball, disc contours and white lines legible. Direction 1: Brazilian heritage premium. Table with beautiful worn walnut wooden rim, satin smooth muted forest green laminate playing surface, warm evening light in a tasteful Brazilian family games room, subtle analog scoreboard and cloth details beyond the table, rich warm amber and cream with teal and coral abstract team discs. Honest tactile manufacturing details, hairline acrylic scratches, paper inserts, collectible nostalgic quality. Closeup disc study shows layered translucent resin, brass-colored printed ring without changing silhouette. Goal celebration is warm golden paper confetti BEHIND the miniature goal, short delicate light pulse outside playing surface. Inviting, sophisticated nostalgia that younger people also want to collect. No sepia filter. Maintain miniature tabletop scale.
